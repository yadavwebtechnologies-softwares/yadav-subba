About Me

My Background, Education and Journey

I am Yadav Subba, a self-taught web developer, content writer and mathematics teacher from Assam, India. My journey has been shaped by education, personal challenges, independent learning and a commitment to creating meaningful work.

My Roots

Background and Family

Where My Journey Began

I was born on 8 September 2003 in Dikhlem Nepali Subba Gaon, Hawaipur, West Karbi Anglong, Assam. This village is where I grew up and developed the values that continue to guide my life. My mother has been a source of quiet strength, while the memory of my late father remains one of my greatest sources of inspiration.

Learning and Growth

Education and Self-Reliance

My School Years

My education began in a local government school, and I completed my schooling entirely within government institutions. I did not have access to coaching centres or private tuition. Instead, I relied on self-discipline, determination and the belief that consistent effort can create opportunities even when resources are limited.

A Difficult Turning Point

In the summer of 2020, my life changed profoundly when my father passed away following a fall from a betel nut tree. He was 55 years old and the sole provider for our family. His loss brought grief, uncertainty and new responsibilities. My mother became the head of our family, and my sister and I learned to navigate circumstances we had never anticipated.

My HSLC Examinations

That same year, I appeared for my HSLC examinations while recovering from a medical condition and preparing with limited resources. Each day, I cycled alone to my examination centre at New Hindi High School along the Dikhlem Raisem Mailoo road. Although many of my friends travelled by motorcycles and other vehicles, I continued on my bicycle, determined to complete my examinations.

I passed with a third division. Before his passing, my father had smiled with pride at my achievement. That memory remains deeply precious to me and continues to remind me of the importance of perseverance.

Higher Secondary Achievement

In 2023, I appeared for my Higher Secondary examinations and achieved 80.4% without coaching or private tuition. This achievement was an important milestone in my education and strengthened my confidence in independent learning.

I was honoured with the Banikanda Kakati Award under the Pragyan Bharati Scheme through the Directorate of Higher Education, Government of Assam, and received a scooty as recognition. It was a proud moment, although I wished my father could have been there to witness it and encourage me to keep moving forward.

Higher Education in English

I have completed my Bachelor of Arts in English Honours, and my results are awaited. Studying English and literature has strengthened my reading, writing, critical thinking and communication skills. These abilities continue to support my interests in content writing, web development and teaching.

Perspective

Personal Growth

Communication and Hearing Impairment

I live with a hearing impairment, which has shaped my experiences and encouraged me to approach communication with greater thoughtfulness and determination. My interest in reading, writing and expressing ideas clearly remains an important part of my personal and professional development.

Education and Problem-Solving

Mathematics and Teaching

Teaching Mathematics

Mathematics is another important part of my life. I teach mathematics to students from Classes 1 to 10, helping them develop conceptual understanding, logical reasoning and confidence in problem-solving.

I believe mathematics is more than a collection of symbols and formulas; it is a way to understand patterns, relationships and the world around us. My aim is to make learning approachable and meaningful for every student.

My Work

Web Development and Writing

A Self-Taught Web Developer

I am a self-taught web developer who continues to develop technical skills through independent learning and practical work. My interests include building useful web-based tools, developing responsive interfaces and creating digital experiences that are accessible and straightforward to use.

I also work with written content, where clarity, structure and meaningful communication are important. My combination of technical learning, writing and teaching allows me to approach problems from different perspectives.

My journey has not been without hardship, but it has taught me resilience, patience and the value of continuing to learn. I remain committed to improving my skills, building useful projects and creating work that has genuine purpose rather than pursuing recognition alone.
