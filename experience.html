Career and Projects

Experience Overview

Job Update Portal

Manager & Lead Developer

2022–2024

For over two years, I managed, maintained, and developed a job update portal designed to connect job seekers with employment opportunities across various sectors. My responsibilities covered frontend development, backend logic, content management, website maintenance, debugging, and user experience optimisation.

Beyond the technical work, I aimed to make the platform a useful resource for people seeking employment. I maintained job-related content, refined website functionality, and worked to make relevant information easier for users to access. This experience strengthened my skills in web development, platform management, troubleshooting, and maintaining a service over an extended period.

Current Status

The website's domain has expired due to financial constraints, and the portal is currently unavailable. I intend to revisit the project when circumstances allow. Although the platform is temporarily inactive, the experience remains an important part of my professional journey and continues to inform my approach to building practical, user-focused web solutions.

Yadav Web Tools

Founder & Lead Developer

2024–Present

I founded and developed Yadav Web Tools, a collection of twelve free, client-side utility tools designed to simplify everyday digital tasks. The collection includes utilities for age calculation, QR code generation, GST calculation, password generation, and other practical needs.

The project follows a privacy-focused, lightweight development approach. Built using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the tools are designed to operate directly in the user's browser without unnecessary frameworks or external dependencies. This approach prioritises simplicity, performance, and a straightforward user experience.

As the founder and lead developer, I focus on implementation, functionality, usability, and maintainable code. The project reflects my commitment to creating practical digital solutions that people can access free of charge.

For me, Yadav Web Tools represents more than a technical project. It is an opportunity to apply my skills to everyday problems and make useful technology more accessible, reliable, and easier to use.
